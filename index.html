Kim Jeonghyeon

M.S. student, Mechanical Engineering, Korea University
Human-Machine Systems Lab, advised by Prof. Shinsuk Park

When a robot's model is wrong, which errors actually matter for its task?

I study how task requirements should shape robot models, estimators, and controllers. On a Unitree G1 without force sensors, I quantify how fast and how accurately the push must be estimated to resist it without stepping. On a Dobot CR3A, I restore contact with an unknown tilted surface while preserving where a drawing lands, and predict how far other corrections displace it. In both, the aim is a model built at the level the task needs, not a more complex one.
