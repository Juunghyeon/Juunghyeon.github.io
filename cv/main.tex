\section{Research Experience}

\researchentry{Push Recovery for a Force-Sensorless Humanoid (Unitree G1)}{Mar. 2025 -- Present}
\compactlist
  \item Developed a pelvis-IMU-based disturbance estimator and an MPC-based hold-or-step controller, investigating how estimation delay and modeling errors affect in-place push recovery.
  \item In simulation, recovered from 65 of 80 push trials versus 62 for a stepping baseline, while taking 49\% fewer steps and recovering from 30 pushes without stepping.
  \item At small delays, each additional 10\,ms of estimation delay reduced the maximum holdable push force by approximately 3.7\%.
  \item Implemented and evaluated the estimator and hold layer on a physical Unitree G1: with stepping disabled, the median maximum lateral push sustained per block was 69\,N with the hold layer versus 0\,N without it.
\finishcompactlist

\researchentry{Intent-Preserving Contact Control on Unknown Surfaces (Dobot CR3A)}{Sep. 2025 -- Present}
\compactlist
  \item Formulated a task-aware contact-control method that preserves task-relevant coordinates while allowing motion in designated yield directions, using only joint sensing.
  \item Developed and tested the controller on a physical Dobot CR3A drawing on tilted boards unknown to the controller: figure displacement was 0.38--0.49\,mm, versus 11.3--25.2\,mm for nearest-point correction supplied with measured surface normals.
\finishcompactlist

\researchentry{Real-Time Robot Communication and Data Infrastructure}{Mar. 2025 -- Aug. 2026}
\compactlist
  \item Developed the system framework and ROS~2 communication architecture for an industry--academia mobile-manipulator project with MOVENSYS, connecting simulation, AI applications, a user interface, and physical robot hardware.
  \item Implemented time-synchronized sensor and motor data collection and edge-computing infrastructure while preserving the robot's real-time control cycle.
\finishcompactlist
