    {Mar. 2024 -- Present}

  \vspace{0.5mm}
  \textbf{Balance-Aware Whole-Body Control for Humanoid Standing Manipulation}
  \resumeItemListStartSub
    \item
    Formulated a \textbf{Balance Demand Descriptor (BDD)} based on
    centroidal dynamics to represent lower-body balance demands induced by
    upper-body motion, payloads, and external interaction.
    \item
    Integrated BDD into a reinforcement-learning locomotion policy to
    distinguish demands manageable within the current stance from those
    requiring corrective stepping.
    \item
    Designed simulation evaluations involving payload variation and
    continuous upper-body disturbances to assess physical validity and
    behavioral utility.
  \resumeItemListEndSub

  \vspace{1.3mm}
  \textbf{Visual Perception for Quadrupedal Object Interaction}
  \resumeItemListStartSub
    \item

    observation and local near-field sensing in a common coordinate frame.
    \item

    occlusion during quadrupedal locomotion.

  \resumeItemListStartSub

    manipulator.
