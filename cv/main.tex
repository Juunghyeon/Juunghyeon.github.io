\section{Research Experience}

\researchentry{Push Recovery for a Force-Sensorless Humanoid (Unitree G1)}{Mar. 2025 -- Present}
\compactlist
  \item Developed a pelvis-IMU disturbance estimator and an MPC-based hold-or-step controller that lets a force-sensorless humanoid decide, from motion alone, whether to hold a push in place or step to recover.
  \item In simulation, held 30 of 80 pushes in place with 49\% fewer recovery steps than a stepping-only baseline.
  \item Validated the hold layer separately on the physical G1 with stepping disabled.
\finishcompactlist

\researchentry{Intent-Preserving Contact Control on Unknown Surfaces (Dobot CR3A)}{Sep. 2025 -- Present}
\compactlist
  \item Formulated contact-rich manipulation on surfaces of unknown orientation as a task-preserving constraint problem, developing a joint-sensing-only controller that lets the task specify which coordinates may yield to the surface.
  \item On a Dobot CR3A drawing on tilted boards, kept figure drift to 0.38--0.48\,mm, versus 11.3--25.2\,mm for a nearest-point baseline given the true surface normal.
\finishcompactlist

\researchentry{Real-Time Robot Communication and Data Infrastructure}{Mar. 2025 -- Aug. 2026}
\detail{Industry--academia project with MOVENSYS. Designed the system framework and ROS~2 communication structure connecting simulation, an LLM, a user interface, and a physical mobile manipulator, with time-synchronized edge data collection that preserves the real-time control cycle.}
